How it works

Four steps.
Everything included.

Token Creator handles all the technical complexity. You just fill in the details.

01 —
Create your token

Choose a name, symbol, and upload your logo. Set the total supply and how much you keep for yourself. Your token is created on Solana in seconds — with a public image, a description, and a permanent address anyone can look up.

"I wanted a token for my Discord server to reward active members. It took me 8 minutes."
Permanent logo on IPFS Fixed or flexible supply Visible on Solscan
02 —
Lock your allocation optional

Keep a portion of the supply for yourself — and prove to your community you won't dump it. Set a lock period (cliff) and a release schedule. Everything is on-chain and visible to anyone.

Your community sees that your tokens unlock gradually over 12 months. Trust goes up.
Cliff period Linear unlock Publicly verifiable
03 —
Add liquidity optional

Make your token tradeable. Pair it with SOL or USDC and set your initial price. Your token appears on DEXscreener, wallets, and price trackers automatically. No listing fees, no gatekeepers.

Once the pool is live, anyone can buy your token directly from their Phantom wallet.
Tradeable on Solana DEXs Appears on DEXscreener You set the price
04 —
Share your token page

Every token gets a public page you can share with your community from day one. It shows the live price, a price chart, the vesting schedule, and the top holders — all pulled automatically from the blockchain.

Share one link. Your community sees the price, who holds what, and that your tokens are locked. That's credibility.

Built for trust

Your tokens.
Your rules.

Token Creator never touches your funds. Every action is signed by your wallet and recorded on-chain.

01
You keep full control

We never hold your tokens or your keys. Every transaction is signed directly from your wallet (Phantom or Solflare).

02
Fixed supply, provably

Revoke the mint authority and no one — including you — can ever create more tokens. Your community can verify it on-chain.

03
No one can freeze wallets

Revoke the freeze authority and your members' wallets can never be blocked. Their tokens are truly theirs.

04
Everything is public

Token supply, holder list, vesting schedule — all visible on-chain. Your token page shows it all, automatically.
