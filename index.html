How it works

Four steps.
One complete launch.

Every tool you need to go from idea to live token with liquidity.

01 —
Create your token

Name, symbol, logo — uploaded to IPFS via Pinata. Minted on-chain as an SPL token with Metaplex metadata. Choose supply, decimals, and dev allocation. Optionally revoke mint and freeze authorities after vesting for a fully trustless token.

SPL Token Metaplex IPFS / Pinata
02 —
Set up vesting optional

Lock your dev allocation using Streamflow, a fully audited vesting protocol on Solana. Configure a cliff period and total duration. Tokens unlock linearly and transparently on-chain.

Streamflow On-chain vesting
03 —
Add liquidity optional

Create a concentrated liquidity pool on Orca Whirlpool. Choose your pair, set an initial price, and deposit. Pool init, tick arrays, SOL wrapping, and position opening are handled automatically.

Orca Whirlpool Full-range position WSOL auto-wrap
04 —
Share your public page

Every token gets a page at /token/[mint] — live price, embedded chart, top holders, supply, and vesting status. All pulled from on-chain metadata automatically.

DEXscreener Live price Vesting status

Why Token Creator

Built for founders,
not developers.

Every technical complexity handled so you can focus on building your community.

01
Non-custodial

Your wallet signs every transaction. We never hold your tokens, keys, or funds at any point.

02
Fully on-chain

Token, metadata, vesting, liquidity — everything on Solana. No off-chain database or centralized dependency.

03
Anti-rug options

Revoke mint authority to fix supply forever. Revoke freeze authority so no wallet can be blocked.

04
IPFS metadata

Token image and metadata pinned to IPFS via Pinata. Decentralized, permanent, accessible to any explorer.

05
Instant DEX listing

Once your Orca pool is live, your token is tradeable and appears on DEXscreener and compatible wallets.

06
Shareable page

Send investors to your token page — live price, chart, vesting timeline, holder distribution.
