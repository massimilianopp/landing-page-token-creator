How it works

Four steps.
Everything included.

Token Creator handles the technical complexity. You focus on your community.

01 —
Create your token

Name, symbol, logo — uploaded permanently to IPFS. Your token is minted on Solana in seconds, with on-chain metadata visible on any explorer. Set the total supply and choose how much you keep.

"I wanted a token to reward active members of my Discord. It took me 8 minutes from zero."
Permanent logo on IPFS Fixed or flexible supply Visible on Solscan
02 —
Lock your allocation optional

Prove to your community you won't dump your tokens. Set a cliff period and a vesting schedule — tokens unlock gradually and transparently on-chain. Anyone can verify it, anytime.

Your community sees your tokens unlock over 12 months. That's the kind of transparency that builds trust.
Cliff period Linear unlock Publicly verifiable
03 —
Add liquidity optional

Make your token tradeable. Pair it with SOL or USDC, set your initial price, and open a liquidity pool on Orca — Solana's main DEX. Your token appears on DEXscreener automatically.

Once the pool is live, anyone can buy your token directly from Phantom or any Solana wallet.
Tradeable on Solana DEXs Listed on DEXscreener You set the price
04 —
Share your token page

Every token gets a public page you can share from day one. It shows the live price, a chart, top holders, and the vesting schedule — all pulled automatically from the blockchain. No setup needed.

One link. Your community sees the price, who holds what, and that your tokens are locked. That's credibility you can share.

Built for trust

Your tokens.
Your rules.

Token Creator never touches your funds. Every action is signed by your wallet and recorded permanently on-chain.

01
You keep full control

We never hold your tokens or your private keys. Every transaction is signed directly from your Phantom or Solflare wallet.

02
Fixed supply, provably

Revoke the mint authority and no one — including you — can ever create more tokens. Your community can verify it on-chain in seconds.

03
No one can freeze wallets

Revoke the freeze authority and your members' wallets can never be blocked. Their tokens are truly theirs — forever.

04
Everything is public

Token supply, holder list, vesting schedule — all visible on-chain. Your token page shows it automatically, no trust required.
